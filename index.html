Campus Overview

Residential Care in Tiruchirappalli

HOPE worldwide Orphanage Trichy is a residential facility located in Tiruchirappalli (Trichy), Tamil Nadu, India. The campus provides long-term living facilities, accommodation, daily dining, study spaces, and outdoor recreation.

The facility is built around an open central courtyard that provides natural daylight and fresh ventilation across multi-level living wings and student corridors.
