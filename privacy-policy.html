Privacy Policy

Document Version: 1.1 • Last Reviewed: September 2026

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the official website and institutional communications of HOPE worldwide Orphanage Trichy, situated in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, India. It explains how information submitted through our contact forms or direct communications is received and handled.

2. Information Collection & Use

We collect personal details only when voluntarily submitted by visitors—such as when requesting a visit, inquiring about campus facilities, or contacting administration. This information may include:

  • Full name and organization/institution affiliation
  • Contact telephone number
  • Email address
  • Inquiry details or message notes

Information provided is used strictly to respond to your inquiry and coordinate administrative appointments. We do not sell, rent, or distribute contact lists to commercial third parties.

3. Safeguarding of Resident Children

The privacy, dignity, and personal safety of children resident at HOPE worldwide Orphanage Trichy are our highest priority.

  • Personal identification records and individual case files of residents are strictly confidential and never published online.
  • Photographic documentation on this website depicts campus environments, facilities, and communal activities under institutional oversight.
  • Visitors to the physical facility are not permitted to capture or publish unauthorized personal photographs or recordings of minors.
