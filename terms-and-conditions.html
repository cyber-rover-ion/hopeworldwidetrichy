Terms and Conditions

Document Version: 1.1 • Applicable to Visitors & Website Users

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing this website or visiting the campus of HOPE worldwide Orphanage Trichy, you agree to observe these Terms and Conditions. These terms are established to protect the safety and dignity of the children in residential care and ensure smooth administrative operations.

2. Campus Visitation Protocol

To ensure the continuous security, order, and well-being of the residents:

  • Prior Appointment Required: All prospective visitors, volunteers, and well-wishers must coordinate directly with the administrative office prior to arriving on campus. Unannounced visits cannot be accommodated.
  • Visitor Verification: Visitors are required to present government-approved photo identification at the administrative office upon arrival.
  • Visiting Hours: Visits are scheduled during administrative hours (Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM) subject to prior arrangement.

3. Photography & Child Safeguarding Rules

In accordance with child safeguarding standards:

  • Visitors are strictly prohibited from taking individual photographs, audio recordings, or video of children without explicit prior written authorization from the Managing Director.
  • Distribution of food items, gifts, or medicine must be routed through administrative staff to maintain dietary safety and hygiene standards.

4. Media & Website Content Rights

All documentary photography, architectural images, text, and logos published on this website are the property of HOPE worldwide Orphanage Trichy. Re-publication, distribution, or commercial use without prior written consent is strictly prohibited.
